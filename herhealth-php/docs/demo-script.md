# Two-minute live demonstration (sections 35–36)

Use a stable build on an ordinary Android phone, `APP_ENV=testing` or `pilot`. No fake statistics or staged functionality.

## Before recording

- [ ] At least one facility genuinely verified (otherwise the referral step shows the honest “No verified services…” message).
- [ ] At least one human-recorded audio lesson uploaded, or explain that the phone's own voice reads the reviewed text.
- [ ] Safety suite re-run after the nurses' decision on SAFETY-021; evidence saved.
- [ ] Clear the phone's HerHealth data (Privacy → Clear) so the privacy introduction appears.

## Sequence

| Time | Show |
| --- | --- |
