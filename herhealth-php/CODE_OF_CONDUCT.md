# Code of Conduct

HerHealth Liberia exists to serve women and girls with dignity. We expect the same of everyone who takes part in the project.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, patient and kind. Assume good intent.
- Welcome people of every background, language, gender, age, ability and level of experience.
- Discuss reproductive and sexual health factually and without shame, stigma or stereotypes.
- Never share anyone's personal or health information, including test participants'.
- Accept constructive feedback and give it graciously.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, sexualised remarks, intimidation, doxxing, or sharing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the SkillBridge Liberia Corporation maintainers. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may warn, suspend or remove contributors who violate this code.
